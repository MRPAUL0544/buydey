Privacy at BuyDey

What information the marketplace uses and how users stay in control.

Information collected

Account details, listings, messages, saved items, device information, reports and activity needed to operate and protect the marketplace.

How information is used

To provide accounts and listings, connect buyers and sellers, personalise results, prevent abuse, answer support requests and improve BuyDey.

Sharing

Public seller and listing information is visible to marketplace users. BuyDey does not sell personal information. Service providers receive only the access necessary to perform their work.

Security and retention

Production data will use access controls, encryption and retention limits. This local preview currently stores demonstration data only in your browser.

Your choices

Users may request access, correction or deletion and may control marketing communications. Contact privacy@buydey.com.

Launch review

This policy is a product foundation, not final legal advice. It must be reviewed for Ghana’s Data Protection Act before public launch. Last updated: 5 August 2026.
