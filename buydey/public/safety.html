BuyDey Protection

Trade confidently

Simple habits make marketplace deals safer for buyers and sellers across Ghana.

Meet safely

Choose a busy public place during daylight. Tell someone where you are going, take a friend when possible, and avoid changing the meeting location at the last minute.

Inspect before paying

Check the item carefully and test important functions. Confirm serial numbers or documents for phones, vehicles and other high-value products.

Protect your money

BuyDey will never ask for your mobile-money PIN, password or one-time code. Never pay an advance fee just to view an item.

Recognise warning signs

  • A price far below normal market value.
  • Pressure to pay immediately or move the conversation elsewhere.
  • A seller refusing inspection or identity verification.
  • Requests for gift cards, crypto, PINs or verification codes.

Report concerns

Use the report option on a listing or contact help@buydey.com. In an emergency, contact the Ghana Police Service.
